# Agent guide — 10_Strategy

This folder holds where we're going: the annual strategy, our active bets, and the risks against them.

When working here:
- Every **bet** note must state a one-sentence thesis and link at least one [[KPI Dashboard]] metric that proves or kills it.
- Every **risk** note must name an exposure *and* a mitigation, and link the bet it threatens.
- [[Annual Strategy]] is the hub — keep its links to bets and risks current.
- Don't invent new strategy here; reflect decisions recorded in [[Decision Log]].
