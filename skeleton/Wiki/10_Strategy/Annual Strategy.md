# Annual Strategy

Your Company's plan for the year — a few deliberate bets, the risks we accept, and the numbers that tell us we're winning.

## Our bets
- [[Bet - Operational Leverage]] — Document every process so the team doubles output without doubling headcount.
- [[Bet - Premium Positioning]] — Win on quality and trust rather than competing on price.
- [[Bet - AI-Native Operations]] — Give agents the same context as staff so routine work runs itself.
- [[Bet - Disciplined Expansion]] — Enter one new market at a time, only after the core is profitable.

## Risks we're watching
- [[Risk - Key-Person Dependency]] — Too much context lives only in founders' heads.
- [[Risk - Margin Compression]] — Input costs rise faster than we can reprice.
- [[Risk - Overextension]] — Growing in too many directions at once.

## How we'll know it's working
Tracked in [[KPI Dashboard]]. Connects back to [[Mission and Vision]].
