# Glossary

The shared language of Your Company. If you use one of these words, mean this:

- **Company Brain** — The single source of truth for how we think, decide, and operate.
- **Decision Log** — A dated record of every consequential choice and why we made it.
- **Playbook** — A repeatable procedure a new hire can follow without supervision.
- **Owner** — The single person accountable for a decision, bet, or risk.
- **Bet** — A deliberate, time-boxed wager of resources against a thesis.
- **North Star** — The one metric that best proxies durable value creation.
